# Mathematical research preprints

A single archive for research preprints and their reproducibility materials. The manuscripts are publicly available working papers, not peer-reviewed publications.

| Preprint | What it establishes | Editable source, code, and certificates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Unbounded branching degree in three-dimensional Gilbert–Steiner flows](gilbert-steiner-unbounded.pdf) | Answers the separate dimension-only question negatively: optimal three-dimensional stars have arbitrarily high degree as the cost exponent approaches one and mass ratios grow. | [Editable proof and audit](gilbert-steiner-unbounded-supplement.zip) |
| [The dimension scale of equal-demand Gilbert–Steiner stars](gilbert-steiner-comparable.pdf) | Proves matching exponential dimension orders when a single source supplies equal-demand sinks and the cost power varies. The upper bound allows any fixed demand ratio. | [Source and proof audit](gilbert-steiner-comparable-supplement.zip) |
| [Demand ratios and branching in Gilbert–Steiner stars](gilbert-steiner-demand-ratio.pdf) | Gives polylogarithmic bounds in the demand ratio at fixed dimension, including square-root logarithmic and logarithmic lower and upper orders in dimension three. | [Source and proof audit](gilbert-steiner-demand-ratio-supplement.zip) |
| [The leading energy correction for irrigation of a sphere](sphere-irrigation-asymptotics.pdf) | Proves the sharp logarithmic correction as the cost exponent tends to one and gives the scale of the mean path detour, in all dimensions including the circle. | [Source and proof audit](sphere-irrigation-asymptotics-source.zip) |
| [Quantized entropy and the near-linear branched transport cost](branched-transport-entropy.pdf) | Gives a law-uniform entropy asymptotic for sphere and parallel-slice transport and determines the sharp coefficient in the uniform case. | [Source and proof audit](branched-transport-entropy-source.zip) |
| [Radius-weighted angular entropy in near-linear branched transport](branched-transport-radial-entropy.pdf) | Gives a uniform small-parameter cost formula for an arbitrary target law in the unit ball, with sharp logarithmic coefficient for uniform volume. | [Complete proof source](branched-transport-radial-entropy-source.zip) |
| [Farey 分支的收敛与逆恢复](tropical-caustic-farey-recovery.pdf) | In the absence of tropical convexity constraints, proves inverse recovery for ordered convergent branches and for unlabelled completed curves from uniform limits. | [Proof source and exact example checks](tropical-caustic-farey-recovery-source.zip) |
| [零长度 Farey 端点集的逆恢复（补充）](tropical-caustic-bare-endpoints.pdf) | Recovers rational jump endpoints from fixed-endpoint secants and proves a robust zero-length family uniquely recoverable even against merely pointwise-convergent branches. | [Polynomial bounds for branching degrees in the Gilbert–Steiner problem](gilbert-steiner-degrees.pdf) | Answers Question 1.2 with fixed-parameter superlinear lower and polynomial upper bounds. | [三维单位立方体中 Hamilton 闭巡回的立方成本](cubic-tsp-cube.pdf) | Bounds the worst optimal cubic tour cost by 11.3137–16.9913; certifies the conjectured sharp value through six points, and for any number of points in four alternating corner blocks. | [Source and exact certificates](cubic-tsp-cube-supplement.zip) |
| [立方体巡回的换边上界与七、八点精确值（补充）](cubic-tsp-cube-joint.pdf) | Shows the cubic tour conjecture is sharp through eight points, proves the fixed-order obstacle, and improves the general upper bound to 16.972503. | [Exact joint-edge and finite-point certificates](cubic-tsp-cube-joint-supplement.zip) |
| [三维立方体巡回的多尺度换边界（补充）](cubic-tsp-cube-multiscale.pdf) | Proves the conjectured cubic cost through nine points and the all-points upper bound 16.40. | [三维立方巡回的十点定理（补充）](cubic-tsp-cube-ten-points.pdf) | Proves the exact worst-case supremum for ten points and a quantitative gap for distinct points. | [三维立方巡回的投影、角簇与覆盖球（补充）](cubic-tsp-cube-regions.pdf) | Excludes broad classes of all-cardinality counterexamples: short two-interval coordinate covers, thin slabs and unequal ten-ball covers. | [三维立方巡回的加权直径球与十一点定理](cubic-tsp-cube-eleven-points.pdf) | Proves the exact eleven-point supremum and the dense-set bound with limiting constant 30/π. | [九根圆柱与单位球相切的半径上界](cylinders-upper-bounds.pdf) | Proves strict upper bounds of 0.6453 and 0.619 for nine and ten cylinders, respectively. The insertion conjecture remains open. | [Upper-bound certificates](cylinders-upper-bounds-supplement.zip) |
| [九柱插入猜想的平行轴剪枝（补充）](cylinders-parallel-pruning.pdf) | At the conjectured radius, a nine-cylinder packing cannot have five parallel axes or eight coplanar axis directions. | [Supplementary certificates](cylinders-parallel-pruning-supplement.zip) |
| [九根切线中的非平行接触边（补充）](cylinders-skew-contact.pdf) | Reduces existence to contact graphs with no nonparallel bridge, lowers the general parameter bound to fifteen, and certifies an excluded two-parameter family of skew contacts. | [Skew-contact certificates](cylinders-skew-contact-supplement.zip) |
| [九根切线的接触图与正交锚边（补充）](cylinders-orthogonal-anchor.pdf) | Reduces the general existence search to at most eleven continuous parameters (nine with an anchor edge fixed) and excludes several neighborhoods of orthogonal triples. | [Replayable certificates](cylinders-orthogonal-anchor-supplement.zip) |

Each supplement contains a README with replay commands. To check the published downloads, run `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS` from the repository root. See the [literature and priority checks](NOVELTY.md) for exact source statements and the scope of each result.

## 中文索引

1. **A7 晶格**：在指定密度下证明 A7 是所有双点周期结构中唯一最优的；并给出能量更低的三点周期结构。
2. **谱图秩**：完整回答原论文第 21 题，并给出两个最优的截尾谱界，是第 20 条证明所用的秩归约。
3. **Graffiti.pc**：三个猜想各有明确反例，并给出了不能再提高的通用系数。
4. **Bernoulli 不等式**：覆盖同一概率区间内的所有实数参数，但只证明最多十个正权重的情况。

5. **谱图最近邻**：完整解决第 20 条猜想。对每个 n ≥ 3，两种距离下都是同一张图唯一最接近。

6. **圆柱密堆积**：给出 8、9、10 根等半径圆柱围住单位球的精确构型，分别证明最大半径大于 0.70215、0.61196 和 0.54515。

7. **圆柱半径上界**：九柱上界从 0.726 降到 0.6453，十柱半径严格小于 0.619。猜想指定的半径上，最多能放的根数只剩 8 或 9。

8. **分支运输**：证明高维最优网络的最大分叉度至少能超线性增长、至多按多项式增长；单源、各终点等需求时，上下界的增长指数一致。

9. **三维分叉无上限**：固定在三维，若允许成本指数和需求量改变，最优网络中一个结点可有任意多条支路。需求量之比受限时，单源星形网络有统一上限。
